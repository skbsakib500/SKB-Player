# Roadmap — SKB-Player

## Released

### ✅ v0.7–v1.0 — Foundation
- In-app navigation, bottom nav, AMOLED theme
- Foreground service + MediaSession (background play)
- Resume, audio track, sleep timer, PiP
- Study mode: bookmarks, notes, screenshots

### ✅ v1.1–v1.4 — Library
- Playlist queue, watch time, full history
- Search, custom icon, AMOLED splash
- Subtitle delay/position/profiles, sort modes
- Pinch zoom, auto-rotate, folders tab, rename/delete

### ✅ v2.0–v2.4 — Pro
- Favorites, watch later, playlists
- Equalizer, AB repeat, theme switch, thumbnails
- Multi-select, batch delete
- External video player intent filter
- TextureView reflection (Media3 compat)
